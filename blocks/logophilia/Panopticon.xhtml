For the first time, the entire legendary Lensmen saga is presented in a single, definitive volume—restored, enriched, and finally complete.

Spanning billions of years and two galaxies, E. E. “Doc” Smith’s Lensmen series is one of the towering achievements of science fiction. This is the granddaddy of space opera: a cosmic chess match between ancient super-races, where entire civilizations are pawns and the fate of sentient life itself hangs in the balance. From the earliest stirrings of galactic conflict to the final, cataclysmic confrontation on Eddore, Smith delivers jaw-dropping scope, superscience wonders, and relentless adventure on a scale that still feels breathtaking today.
