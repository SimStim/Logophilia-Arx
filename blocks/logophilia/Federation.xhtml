Federation—the complete Terro-human future history collection. H. Beam Piper’s masterful vision of humanity’s destiny among the stars.

Spanning over three thousand years of future history, this definitive volume brings together the complete core of H. Beam Piper’s legendary Terro-Human Future History, one of the most ambitious and internally consistent future histories ever written in science fiction.

From the earliest days of humanity’s expansion into the galaxy, through the golden age of the Federation, the collapse into barbarism, and far beyond, these stories trace the recurring rise and fall of civilizations with intelligence, wit, and gripping adventure.

This Logophilia edition includes: Uller Uprising; Four-Day Planet; The Cosmic Computer; Space Viking; The Edge of the Knife; A Slave is a Slave; Ministry of Disturbance; The Return; The Keeper; Omnilingual; Naudsonce; Oomphel in the Sky; Graveyard of Dreams.

Arranged in in-universe chronological order so the grand sweep of Piper’s universe unfolds naturally as you read.
