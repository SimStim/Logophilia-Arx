After a carriage accident leaves her convalescing in the isolation of Styria, Laura finds herself drawn to an unexpected companion. Carmilla arrives like a fever dream: magnetic, beautiful, and possessed of a familiarity that defies logic. But as their intimacy deepens within the castle’s quiet halls, the slow decay of dread begins to seep into the surrounding valley. Young women are falling into slow, inexplicable decay, their lives slipping away amid night terrors and sudden exhaustion. As the line between affection and suspicion blurs, Laura must confront a truth far more predatory than any mere stranger: some shadows do not merely haunt us; they hunger for us.
