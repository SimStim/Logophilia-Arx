Abstract

Readers inclined to proceed directly to the Prologue may do so without missing any essential information.

This account explores aspects of the human condition during the early 22nd century. The events presented here have been deemed suitable for examining themes of resilience, integrity, bonding, loss, and a wide spectrum of related aspects. They also shine a light on social and group dynamics during crises. Focus on the emotional landscape has been maintained throughout.

Ancillary media does exist, including an aural rendering of the events by a contemporary artist, accessible at lphi.top/p-hubrisost.

Readers are invited to share observations and insights by accessing the review board at lphi.top/hubris. Feedback may be addressed by the author directly.

In anticipation of fascinating discussions, the author.
