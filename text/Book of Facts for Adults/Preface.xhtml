 Non-fiction Trivia Preface

Inspired by Isaac Asimov’s beloved Book of Facts, this collection aims to spark curiosity and wonder in young minds—just like it did for me as a teen!

Like Asimov’s original, you’ll find a wealth of fascinating trivia within these pages. But this book has been updated for a new generation. We’ve carefully fact-checked every detail with human researchers, and focused on presenting information that remains relevant—even as the world changes. For example, instead of specific distances that might become outdated, we’ll tell you about Voyager 1 being the farthest object humans have sent into space. This edition also includes a new chapter exploring Human Reproduction and Sexual Physiology, presented with sensitivity and accuracy.

My hope is that these facts will entertain and inspire readers of all ages—and perhaps even ignite a lifelong love of learning!

—The Author
