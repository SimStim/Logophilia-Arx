 Non-fiction Trivia
Medicine and Health

Research suggests that people with blood type O are more attractive to mosquitoes than those with blood type A, B, or AB.

The human brain has a storage capacity estimated to be equivalent to approximately 2.5 petabytes (or a million gigabytes) of digital memory.

There are more bacterial cells in your body than human cells. The bacterial cells outnumber human cells by about 10 to 1.

On average, the human heart beats around 100,000 times per day, pumping approximately 2,000 gallons (7,570 liters) of blood. This totals about 2.5 billion times over an average lifespan.

Extended periods without sleep can lead to hallucinations. The record for the longest time without sleep is reportedly 11 days, after which severe psychological effects can occur.

Fingernails grow faster than toenails, with an average growth rate of about 3.5 millimeters per month for fingernails compared to 1.6 millimeters per month for toenails.

The heaviest human brain ever recorded weighed 2,850 grams (6 pounds 4.5 ounces). Dutch pathologist Gerard Christiaan van Walsem measured it during the autopsy of an unnamed young man at Meerenberg Asylum in 1899 near Haarlem, Netherlands. The average human brain weighs about 1,350 grams, making this brain more than twice the usual size. Walsem’s notes indicate the patient suffered from epilepsy and intellectual disabilities, and died at 21 years old. However, brain dissection and preservation methods were not fully standardized then, possibly slightly exaggerating its size due to methodological differences.

Human bones are about five times stronger than steel of the same density. A cubic inch of bone can bear a load of approximately 19,000 pounds (8,626 kilograms) before crushing.

The liver uniquely regenerates itself after injury, capable of returning to normal size even if up to 90% has been removed.

The human diploid genome, present in most cells, comprises approximately 6 billion DNA base pairs. Unwound, this spans about 2 meters, but typically it coils tightly around histone proteins like miniature balls of kite string. With an estimated 37 trillion cells in the body, the total length of DNA reaches approximately 74 trillion meters or 74 billion kilometers. This distance is about 12 times the average Earth-Pluto distance of 5.9 billion kilometers.

In an average adult, the human heart beats about 70 to 85 times per minute, with gender differences noted. Adult males average 70 to 72 beats per minute, while females average 78 to 82. This discrepancy is due to the smaller size of the female heart, which pumps less blood per beat than the larger male heart, necessitating a higher rate to maintain comparable output.

According to the American Lung Association, The speed of a human sneeze can exceed 100 miles per hour (160 kilometers per hour) and create upwards of 100,000 droplets.

Tooth enamel, the hardest substance in the human body, is primarily made up of minerals. Despite its strength, it can erode over time due to acids from foods and bacteria. Teeth are also the only part of the human body that can’t repair themselves. Once tooth enamel is eroded or damaged, it cannot regenerate.

Even identical twins have different fingerprints. Fingerprints are formed randomly during fetal development and remain unchanged throughout a person’s life. Fingerprints are not just unique to humans but are also unique to each finger on an individual.

In the pharmaceutical industry, the color of pills can affect how patients perceive their effectiveness. For example, red and orange pills are often associated with stimulating effects, while blue and green pills are seen as calming.

The placebo effect is a powerful phenomenon where patients show improvement in their condition after receiving a placebo—a treatment with no therapeutic effect.

Laughter has been shown to have health benefits, including reducing stress hormones and increasing immune cells and infection-fighting antibodies.

On average, a person blinks about 15–20 times per minute, which amounts to about 1,200 times per hour and 28,800 times in a day.

The preference for sweet tastes is innate from birth, while preferences for other tastes like sour and bitter develop later in life.

Research indicates that people with red hair perceive pain differently than others. They may be more sensitive to certain pain types and often need higher doses of painkillers. However, studies also suggest they might have a higher overall pain tolerance.

Humans are the only mammals that willingly delay sleep. Other mammals sleep when they are tired and wake when they are rested.

Humans can hear sounds ranging from about 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz, but this range diminishes with age, especially for high-frequency sounds.

The human body maintains a remarkably constant core temperature of around 98.6°F (37°C), regardless of external temperatures.

According to the National Institutes of Health (DOI: 10.1126/science.1249168), the human nose can distinguish between at least 1 trillion different scents.

If you were to lay out all the blood vessels in an adult body end to end, they would stretch about 60,000 miles (96,560 kilometers).

Pain is subjective and influenced by psychological factors. For example, distraction can reduce the perceived intensity of pain.

Humans shed about 600,000 particles of skin every hour, which amounts to about 1.5 pounds (0.7 kilograms) of skin each year.

Hiccups occur due to involuntary contractions of the diaphragm muscle and can be triggered by eating too quickly, drinking carbonated beverages, or sudden changes in temperature.

The muscles that control your eyes are the most active in your body and are capable of moving your eyes up to 100,000 times in a day.

During REM (Rapid Eye Movement) sleep, your body is typically paralyzed to prevent you from acting out your dreams. This paralysis involves most skeletal muscles and excludes muscles that help us breathe, digest, and some muscles of the eyes.

Over a lifetime, the average person produces 25,000 litres of saliva, enough to fill two swimming pools.

Studies like doi: 10.1186/s12865-023-00565-0 have shown that exposure to dirt and bacteria early in life can help strengthen the immune system and reduce the risk of allergies and autoimmune diseases.

The human digestive system can break down and digest almost any food material, including cellulose in plants.
