A Note on the Arrangement

To open the works of H. Beam Piper is to step into something far greater than a collection of stories. It is to enter the Terro-Human Future History: a vast, meticulously crafted tapestry of human destiny spanning more than three thousand years.

In this volume we have deliberately set aside the usual conventions of anthology-making. Rather than ordering the stories by publication date or alphabetically, we present them in in-universe chronological order.

This choice honors the grand architectural vision underlying Piper’s work. Though each tale stands alone as a complete and satisfying narrative, together they form fragments of a much larger mosaic. By following the internal timeline, the reader is invited to experience the full sweep of humanity’s long journey through the stars:

From the raw, hopeful energy of early expansion, through the zenith and eventual decline of the Federation, into the chaotic and lawless years of the Interregnum, and finally to a distant, haunting echo far in the future.

This chronological arrangement transforms the book from a simple anthology into something closer to a saga. It reveals the great recurring themes of Piper’s universe: the fragile rise of civilization, the slow decay of institutions, and humanity’s eternal struggle against the returning tides of barbarism.

We hope this ordering offers more than clarity; that it serves as a true guide through one of the most ambitious and internally consistent future histories ever written in science fiction.
